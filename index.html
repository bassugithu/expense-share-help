GUIDE 01

Create a Group

Create a dedicated group for roommates, trips, friends, or any shared expenses.

  1. Open Groups. Go to the Groups area from the app.
  2. Choose Create Group. Enter the group name and continue.
  3. Add members. Add the people who will share expenses.
  4. Share the group code. Other members can use the code to join.

GUIDE 02

Join a Group

Use the group information provided by the group creator to join.

  1. Open the Groups area.
  2. Select Join Group.
  3. Enter the group code.
  4. Confirm and open the group.

GUIDE 03

Add an Expense

Add a shared expense and let Expense Share calculate each person's share.

  1. Enter the amount. Type the total expense.
  2. Select Paid By. Choose the member who paid.
  3. Select members. Choose everyone who should share the expense.
  4. Add the expense. Save it to the group.

GUIDE 04

Expense History

Review the group's expenses whenever you need to check previous payments.

  1. Open the group's Expense Log.
  2. Find the expense you want to review.
  3. Check the payer, total amount, and member shares.

GUIDE 05

Settlement

See the simplified balance for the group and understand who needs to pay whom.

  1. Open the group's Settlement tab.
  2. Review each member's balance.
  3. Follow the displayed payment relationships.
  4. After payment, update the settlement according to your group's process.
